1. Overview

This page provides research data and supporting material for a study of solar photovoltaic expansion and hydropower variability in Italy. The collection includes Italian energy observations, Mediterranean climate reference series and a comparative national reference for China.

3. Original Data Sources

The workbook lists the data sources and original table references. Selected providers are listed below.

  • Terna: Italian electricity statistics
  • Eurostat: annual electricity and heat production
  • CSIC: SPEIbase 2.11
  • Copernicus Marine: Mediterranean sea surface temperature
  • Copernicus Marine: Mediterranean mean sea level
  • National Bureau of Statistics of China: 2024 annual release

4. Citation and Data Use

When using these materials, cite the associated manuscript and the original data providers identified in the supplementary material.
